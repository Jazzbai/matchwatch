MATCHWATCH
PRODUCT + BUSINESS OVERVIEW
OCTOBER 20, 2026
SPORTS COMMUNITIES, CONNECTED

Find the game.
Keep the moments.

MatchWatch connects sports fans with games, places to watch, and independent creators. Built first around venue discovery and fan engagement, it is expanding into personal live broadcasts, private recordings, and a library of favorite match videos.

Discover the game → share a watch link → save the recording.

THE PLATFORM TODAY

Discovery: sports schedules, venues, watch parties, reservations, and match-linked YouTube recaps.
Stream: account-owned dashboards, GoPro publishing credentials, watch pages, private video uploads, recording saves, and YouTube favorites.

WHO IT SERVES

Fans finding games and their crowd.
Creators and teams broadcasting games and keeping recordings.
Sports venues organizing match-day activity and reaching relevant fans. Initial pilot focus: Texas sports communities.

WORKING BUSINESS MODEL

Free discovery and public live viewing today. Proposed revenue: venue tools and promotion, creator storage and future AI tools, and later per-match or monthly channel access. Pricing and demand remain to be validated; streaming billing is not implemented.

BUILT VS. NEXT

Self-hosted MediaMTX and MinIO integration passed a real encoder-to-replay test. Account isolation and uploads were also tested. Production configuration and a physical GoPro test remain. Hosting, bandwidth, and storage still carry costs; no managed-streaming plan is required.
